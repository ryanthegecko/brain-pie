About Brain Pie Chart

Brain Pie is a multi-layered visual system to help organize a busy mind. Create Categories for life areas, Slices for specific domains, and Spokes for tasks or reminders. Spokes can be simple reminders, one-time tasks, recurring events, or containers for multiple actions—whatever fits your workflow.
